PREMIUM ECOMMERCE WEBSITE

This ZIP contains one standalone responsive website demo.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

How to run:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

Notes:
- Google Fonts and remote Unsplash images require an internet connection.
- Text, product data, prices, email addresses, and metrics are demo placeholders.
- Connect your own payment provider, backend, or email service before production use.
